What Is Fleet Bikes?

Fleet Bikes is a smart bicycle-sharing solution tailored for university campuses. Students can pick up a bike from any docking station, ride across campus, and drop it off at another station — all using a simple mobile app.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I sign up for Fleet Bikes?

+

Download the Fleet Bikes app from the App Store or Google Play, register with your student ID, and you're ready to ride! The whole process takes less than 2 minutes.

What if I damage a bike?

+

Report any damage through the app immediately. Normal wear and tear is covered, but significant damage may incur additional charges. We recommend taking photos before each ride.

Can I reserve a bike in advance?

+

Premium subscribers can reserve bikes up to 15 minutes in advance through the app. Casual riders operate on a first-come, first-served basis.

What happens if no docking station is available?

+

The app shows real-time availability at all stations. If your destination station is full, the app will suggest the nearest alternative station with available spots.

Are helmets provided?

+

While we encourage safe riding, helmets are not currently provided with bikes. We recommend bringing your own helmet for maximum safety.
