What Is Fleet Bikes?

Fleet Bikes is a smart bicycle-sharing solution tailored for university campuses. Students can pick up a bike from any docking station, ride across campus, and drop it off at another station — all using a simple mobile app.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about riding, paying, and staying safe with Fleet Bikes.

Using the Bikes

Download the Fleet Bikes app, verify with your student ID email, add a payment method, and you can unlock your first ride in under two minutes.

Monthly and semester pass holders get a 15-minute reservation window. Casual riders are first-come-first-serve, but the app highlights docks with high availability.

Each ride session includes 45 minutes of pedal time. Need longer? End the ride at a dock, wait 2 minutes, and unlock again so everyone gets a chance.

The app shows real-time dock capacity. If a station fills up, it automatically suggests two nearby docks with empty slots and pauses billing for the detour.

Tracking, Safety & Policies

Every Fleet Bike has GPS and Bluetooth beacons. Your ride history shows routes, distance, and calories while our ops team monitors anomalies for added safety.

Helmets are strongly recommended. Bring your own or rent one at the Sports Complex kiosk. Riders without helmets may be denied participation during safety drives.

Report issues in the app with photos. Minor wear is covered, but intentional damage or unreported loss may incur up to ₹2,500 plus replacement of accessories.

Docks run 24/7, but patrol support operates 6 AM – 11 PM. Holding a bike past 60 minutes adds ₹20 per 15-minute block to encourage quick returns.

If you leave belongings in the basket, log a ticket within 2 hours. Our maintenance crew collects items during dock sweeps and stores them at the mobility desk.

Payments & Support

We accept UPI, cards, and campus wallet. Unused ride credits are refundable within 7 days, and semester passes can be paused once per term for academic travel.
